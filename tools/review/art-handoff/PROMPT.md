# Prompts for re-rendering Goku's art

Paste one prompt with its reference picture. Ask for a **PNG with a transparent background, 1024 × 1024**.
Send the results back and I'll fit them into Goku: sized, compressed, and checked in light and dark mode.

## 1. Icons (goku-icons-sheet.png)

> Re-render each of these 17 app icons as a polished, professional mobile-app icon. Keep each subject,
> its colors and the bold dark outline, but give it depth: soft shading, clean highlights, crisp edges.
> Simple enough to read at 28 pixels. Dragon Ball style where it fits: Kame House, Master Roshi's turtle
> shell, a four-star Dragon Ball, Korin's Senzu pouch, the Flying Nimbus, Goku's 悟 gi symbol, a ki blast.
> One icon per image, centered, transparent background, no text.

## 2. Character avatars (goku-faces-current.png)

> Draw Goku, Vegeta, Piccolo and Master Roshi as round avatar portraits in a clean Dragon Ball anime style
> (head and shoulders, front-facing, a flat colored circle behind them, bold outlines, cel shading). Also draw
> Goku asleep (eyes closed, little Zs). One character per image, 1024 × 1024, transparent outside the circle.
> If they turn out well, the other 14 fighters are: Gohan, Krillin, Trunks, Bulma, Tien, Yamcha, Frieza, Cell,
> Majin Buu, Android 18, Broly, Beerus, Whis and Jiren.

## 3. Setup coach (goku-mascot-current.png)

> Kid Goku riding the Flying Nimbus cloud, waving, friendly, clean Dragon Ball anime style with bold outlines
> and cel shading. Full body on the cloud, transparent background, 1024 × 1024. If possible, also make
> thinking and cheering versions in the same style.

## 4. Shenron (goku-shenron-current.png)

> Shenron, the Eternal Dragon from Dragon Ball, rising in an S-curve out of the seven glowing Dragon Balls.
> Clean anime style, bold outlines, cel shading, glowing balls at the bottom. Transparent background,
> 1024 × 1024, portrait framing so it fits a phone card.
